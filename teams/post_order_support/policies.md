# Saffron Basket: post-order support policies

_Version 1.0. Saffron Basket is a fictional grocery delivery app, used to show how a second team
onboards to aiquality. The policies below are the only ones the support agent may apply._

## P1 — Missing or damaged items
- Report a missing or damaged item **within 48 hours of delivery**, with a photo in the app.
- The item is then refunded. After 48 hours it can't be refunded.

## P2 — Cancelling an order
- An order can be cancelled **free of charge before it is dispatched**.
- Once it is dispatched (out for delivery), it can't be cancelled.

## P3 — Late delivery
- If an order arrives **more than 30 minutes after the promised time**, a **₹50 credit** is added to the wallet.

## P4 — Refund timing
- Refunds go to the Saffron Basket wallet **instantly**, or to the original payment method in
  **5–7 business days**.

---
### Not covered (hand over to a person)
Delivery partners' or other customers' contact details, price matching, product recommendations,
complaints about a delivery partner's conduct, and anything not in P1–P4.
