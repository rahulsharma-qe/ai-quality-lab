# Meridian Airways — Policy Knowledge Base (AeroAssist)
_The source of truth AeroAssist answers from. Golden-dataset `retrieved_policy` fields reference these IDs._
_Version 1.0 · anything outside these 8 policies + the booking tool = out-of-scope → abstain + escalate._

## P1 — Refund / Cancellation
- Full refund if cancelled **within 24 hours** of booking (boundary inclusive: exactly 24h qualifies).
- After 24 hours: **no full refund**; only applicable taxes/statutory charges are refundable.
- "Full refund" = the full amount paid, including fees and taxes.
- Refund is **initiated** within 24 hours of cancellation; it may take **5–7 business days** to reflect in the customer's account.

## P2 — Baggage: Cabin
- Economy: **1 cabin bag up to 7 kg**.
- Business: **2 cabin bags up to 10 kg total**.
- Oversized/overweight cabin bags must be checked in.

## P3 — Baggage: Check-in
- Economy free allowance: **15 kg**. Business: **30 kg**.
- Excess baggage: **₹500 per kg** over the free allowance.

## P4 — Web Check-in
- Opens **48 hours** before departure, closes **2 hours** before departure.
- After close, check-in only at the airport counter.

## P5 — Date Change / Rescheduling
- Allowed up to **4 hours** before departure.
- Charges = a **change fee + any fare difference**.

## P6 — No-show
- No refund; ticket is forfeited.
- Rebooking only at **full current fare**.

## P7 — Flight Cancellation by Airline
- Passenger's choice of a **full refund** OR **free rebooking** on the next available flight.
- No penalty to the passenger.

## P8 — Special Assistance
- Wheelchair / medical assistance must be requested **at least 48 hours** before departure.
- Requests inside 48 hours are best-effort and not guaranteed.

---
### Out-of-scope (NOT covered — abstain + escalate)
Pets/animals in cabin, visa/immigration, seat selection, in-flight meals, loyalty/points, lounge access,
weather, competitor comparisons, and anything not in P1–P8.
